Altitude span—

How many degrees of altitude the image covers, top edge to bottom. Guessed from the aspect ratio, which is right whenever the panorama was rendered with square degrees — this app's always is.

Top edge sits at—

The band is almost never centred on the horizon — the 2026-09-23 build covers −18° to +79°, whose middle is +30.5°. The report records it exactly; without it, set the top edge by eye.

Keep this much sky6.0°

How far ABOVE the traced skyline the cut is made. The detector errs low and eats into rooflines and treetops; a band of real sky left in is far better than a bite taken out of the thing you are trying to line up. Costs you the stars in that band.

The azimuth offset is the correction to apply to the survey. Put it into the project's azimuthOffsetDeg and every bearing in the profile moves with it.

Where you are

East longitude is positive. A tenth of a degree of longitude is a tenth of a degree of sky, so get this right before blaming the panorama.

When

The sky turns a degree every four minutes, so anything faster than this is work for nothing — and on a phone held in the cold it is battery for nothing.
